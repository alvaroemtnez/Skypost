# 0022. Logo: the sky dome

Status: accepted (2026-11-10).

## Context

The launcher icon was a placeholder (an antenna mast with radiating arcs): thin
strokes, a generic broadcast sign, unreadable at small sizes. Two rounds of
sketches were compared (bubble with a skywave, NVIS bounce, air-mail stamp,
letter, kite, inverted-V, QSL card and others), drawn the way MeshConnect's
icon is: one flat seed colour, one white mark, cut-outs instead of shading.

## Decision

- **Mark:** a speech bubble whose top is a dome (the sky), with a station and
  two upward waves cut out of it. It says "family chat" first and radio second.
- **Seed colour:** Night sky `#26386E`. It is the launcher background and the
  seed of the generated Material scheme (`android/scripts/gen_colors.py`).
- **Alternatives:** Dusk violet `#5A3E96`, Ionosphere `#1F6F8B` and Ember
  `#B4532A`, offered later by a setting that changes the home icon. Night sky
  stays the default. Teal is avoided so the app is not confused with MeshConnect.
- **Centring:** on the area centroid, then 1 unit up on the 108 grid. The round
  top carries little visual weight, so a bounding-box-centred dome looked low.
- **Size:** 40 dp wide on the 108 dp adaptive grid, as in MeshConnect.

## Consequences

- `android/scripts/gen_logo.py` (shapely) writes every asset from one shape:
  launcher foreground, themed (monochrome) layer, splash icon, status-bar icon
  `ic_stat_skypost`, `docs/icon.png` and `docs/logo.png` (README header). Never
  edit the path data by hand.
- The app's fallback colour scheme (Android 8-11) and the splash colours follow
  the new seed.
